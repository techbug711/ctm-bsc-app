1. Overview

PUDO WMS is a warehouse management system used to track every parcel that arrives at, is stored in, and leaves a PUDO (pick-up / drop-off) outlet. It records where each parcel is placed (rack / level / slot), who handled it, and when.

Parcels come from two platforms, identified by their tracking number (AWB) prefix:

  • SPX — Shopee Express. AWB starts with SPXMY (17 characters).
  • YOYI — J&T Express. AWB starts with 680 (15 digits).

The system is a web app that runs in any modern browser (Chrome recommended). It works on desktop, tablet, and phone. Data is stored in the cloud (Firebase) and syncs across all devices in near real time.

2. Logging In

When you open the app, you are shown a staff login screen. Tap your name and enter your personal PIN.

Staff PINs Each staff member has their own PIN. Do not share PINs. The Admin login has extra powers (e.g. deleting parcels) and is shown separately at the bottom of the login screen.

After logging in, your name appears in the top bar and the main interface unlocks. To end your shift, tap End Shift.

Figure 2 — The staff login screen. Tap your name and enter your PIN.

4. IN — Scanning Parcels In

This is the most-used screen. It assigns each incoming parcel to the next available slot.

4.1 Setting the target slot

At the top of the IN screen are four fields that determine where the next parcel goes:

  • Rack — a letter (e.g. A, B, H).
  • Level — a number (e.g. 1, 2).
  • Slot — the individual slot number within that rack/level.
  • Max/Slot — the capacity of each slot (default 15).

The large blue number in the centre shows the next target location (e.g. H-2-4). Below it, the system shows how many items are already in that slot (e.g. "Item 10 of 15 currently in slot").

4.2 Understanding rack / level / slot

Every parcel is stored at a precise address made of three parts:

Figure 3 — A parcel's address is Rack (letter) + Level (number) + Slot (number).

4.2 Scanning a parcel

1
Ensure the target slot is correct (adjust Rack/Level/Slot if needed).
2
Scan the barcode using the camera scanner, or type/paste the AWB into the input box and press Enter.
3
The system validates the AWB and saves it to the target slot. A green confirmation appears.
4
When a slot reaches its capacity, the system plays a chime and automatically advances to the next slot.

4.3 Using the camera scanner

Tap 📷 Start Continuous Scanner to enable the camera. Point it at the barcode — it detects and saves automatically, with a short cooldown (~1.5s) between scans. Tap 🛑 Stop Scanner to turn it off.

4.4 Scan feedback

ResultMeaning
✅ Saved to [slot]Parcel successfully stored.
🚨 Duplicate scanThis AWB is already in the system (shows its existing location).
⚠️ Invalid barcodeThe AWB format is wrong (must start with 680 or SPXMY).
❌ Failed to saveNetwork problem — the system auto-retries in the background.

4.5 Scan log

Tap 📋 Recent Scans to open the scan log, which records every scan attempt with its timestamp, staff member, and result. You can filter by AWB and view Today / Last 50 / Last 100 / Last 500 / All (7 days).

5. OUT — Retrieving Parcels

Use this screen when a customer comes to collect their parcel(s).

5.1 Searching

1
Type at least 4 digits of the tracking number (or the full AWB) and press Enter.
2
Matching parcels appear. You can add multiple search terms to build a list.

5.2 Auto-found (multi-parcel customers)

When a customer has multiple parcels, the system automatically finds and groups all parcels belonging to the same customer name. These extra parcels are marked with an Auto-Found badge.

Important Grouping is based on the exact customer name (case-sensitive). If a customer's name was typed differently on different days, their parcels may not group automatically — search each parcel individually in that case.

5.3 Collecting

Tap 📦 COLLECT ALL DISPLAYED to hand over all currently displayed parcels at once, or collect parcels individually. Collected parcels move from active inventory into history.

6. STOCK — Inventory Dashboard

This screen shows the full active inventory as cards. Each card shows the parcel's location, tracking number, platform badge, customer name, who scanned it in, and when.

6.1 Filters

Use the filter buttons to narrow the view:

  • ALL — every active parcel.
  • SPX — Shopee Express parcels only.
  • YOYI — J&T parcels only.
  • 🚨 RTS — parcels flagged for return to sender (T+6 or older).

6.2 Search & sort

Search by location (e.g. H-2) or tracking number. Sort by Rack (A–Z), Oldest First, or Newest First.

6.3 Card actions

Each card has action buttons:

  • 🔄 Move to New Slot — relocate the parcel (see next section).
  • 📦 Move to RTS BOX / 🚚 Hand to Courier — RTS workflow (see RTS section).
  • 🗑️ Admin Delete — Admin only. Removes the parcel to history.

7. Moving a Parcel to a New Slot

If a parcel is in the wrong slot, or you need to reorganise, use the move feature.

1
On the STOCK tab, find the parcel and tap 🔄 Move to New Slot.
2
A modal opens showing the parcel's AWB and current location.
3
Pick the new Rack, Level, and Slot from the dropdowns.
4
The modal shows the target slot's current occupancy (e.g. "Item 3 of 15").
5
Tap ✅ Move to confirm.
Full-slot warning If the target slot is already full, the modal shows a red SLOT FULL warning and asks you to confirm before overfilling. Only proceed if you are sure.

8. RTS — Return to Sender

Parcels that have been on the shelf too long (T+6 or older) must be returned to the courier. The RTS workflow has two steps:

1
📦 Move to RTS BOX — physically place the parcel in the RTS transit box and tap this. The parcel's location becomes RTS-BOX.
2
🚚 Hand to Courier — when the courier collects it, tap this. The parcel is removed from active inventory and recorded in history.

Parcels flagged for RTS show a red T+6 (RTS) badge on their card.

9. MULTI — Bulk Consolidation

Some customers have 3 or more parcels scattered across different slots. This screen detects those customers so you can consolidate their parcels into the dedicated BULK-RACK, freeing up standard slots.

1
Tap 🔄 Scan Shelves.
2
The system lists customers with many parcels.
3
Move those parcels to the BULK-RACK as directed.

10. HIST — History & Undo

This screen shows the last 50 collected parcels. Each entry shows the tracking number, customer, which rack it came from, who collected it, and when.

10.1 Deep search

Use the search box to find a specific historical AWB (searches the full history, not just the last 50).

10.2 Undo a collection

If a parcel was collected by mistake, tap ↩️ Undo on its history entry. The parcel is restored to active inventory.

11. SLOTS — Slot Occupancy Map

This tab shows a visual map of every rack, level, and slot, with the current item count in each.

1
Open the 🗄️ SLOTS tab. The map loads automatically.
2
Tap 🔄 Sync to refresh the numbers manually.

Each cell shows the slot number, the current count, and the capacity (e.g. 10 / 15). Colours indicate how full the slot is (see the legend below).

Cost note The SLOTS tab does not stream live data. It loads once when you open the tab and refreshes only when you tap Sync — keeping API usage low.

12. Admin Dashboard

The admin dashboard (a separate screen) provides a live operational overview for management:

  • KPIs — total active inventory, scanned in today, collected out today, and RTS hitlist count.
  • Periodic volume matrix — inbound/outbound for today, this week, and this month.
  • Platform split — SPX vs YOYI doughnut chart.
  • Staff leaderboard — today's inbound scans and outbound collections per staff.
  • RTS hitlist — table of parcels needing return to sender.
  • Live slot occupancy — the same colour-coded slot map as the SLOTS tab.

14. Troubleshooting & FAQ

Q: A scan is slow or stuck.

Check the device's Wi-Fi connection. The system needs a stable connection to save each scan. If a scan fails, it auto-retries in the background.

Q: I scanned a parcel but it says "Duplicate scan".

The AWB is already in the system. The message shows its existing location. Do not scan it again — check the existing slot.

Q: A customer's multiple parcels didn't auto-group on OUT.

Grouping matches the exact customer name. If the name was typed differently (e.g. different capitalisation), search each parcel individually.

Q: The slot map shows a slot as empty but there's a parcel there.

Tap 🔄 Sync to refresh. The map is not live — it only updates when you open the tab or tap Sync.

Q: I moved a parcel to the wrong slot.

Use 🔄 Move to New Slot again to correct it.

Q: I collected a parcel by mistake.

Go to the HIST tab, find the parcel, and tap ↩️ Undo.

Q: The camera scanner won't start.

Allow camera permission in the browser. If it still fails, type the AWB manually into the input box.
